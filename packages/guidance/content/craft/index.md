# Directing craft

Craft guides describe methods that can serve multiple formats: interpreting references, forming a treatment, choosing production relationships, designing images and actions, continuity, sound, captions, editing and observing the finished work.

The initial migration retains the existing substantial guides and their examples. They are not split into empty new manuals. Formats point to the relevant craft, and stage guidance reuses marked excerpts from these same files. Model-reference compiler instructions have moved to `rikoko_guide {topic:"models/prompt-writing"}`.

New craft material should explain the decision, available evidence, alternative techniques, applicability and how to observe the result. Link to a format for whole-work structure and to model writing for special syntax; avoid copying the same method into every format.

For speech intention, gaze, gesture and cutaways read `rikoko_guide {topic:"craft/presenter-performance"}`. For product contact, material behavior and adapting effects after a replacement read `rikoko_guide {topic:"craft/product-staging"}`. Both identify the sourced lesson separately from original applications and model success claims.

Before designing new images, read `rikoko_guide {topic:"craft/image-creation"}` for coordinated styling, framing, palette and light, reference production routes, and three complete authored examples. Use these choices in the first visual plan and image prompts.

For step-by-step presenter images, two-view layouts, pose and camera references, and symptom-based visual repairs read `rikoko_guide {topic:"craft/image-direction"}`. Its external workflow examples are conditional methods, not claims of supported model controls or tested outcomes.
