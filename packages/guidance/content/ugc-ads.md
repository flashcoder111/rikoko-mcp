# UGC advertisements

Default workflow: one quick check of critical images, no source-video or final AI review gate, and no automatic quality regeneration. Review criteria below guide planning and optional user-requested evaluation. Deliver technically valid output directly; preserve unreviewed cases honestly. See delivery policy (`rikoko_guide {topic:"visual-review"}`).
Use when an everyday creator encounter and believable product use carry the ad. UGC is a presentation style, not proof of a real customer testimonial. Preserve product facts; do not fabricate personal results, reviews, measured improvements or offers.

## Whole-film treatment

Start with a recognizable audience situation and something the product can visibly demonstrate. A useful structure is a concrete hook, one use, an observable payoff, then product and approved CTA. Adapt it to the brief; neither four shots nor a fixed duration is mandatory. Put proof near the claim it supports. A decorative montage cannot establish a physical result.

A compact product-use ad might establish the creator holding the product, show a single use, and hold the result while the composited CTA arrives. Multi-stage mechanisms may need separate state anchors or deliberate cuts. Leave time to understand the payoff; do not conceal a failed required action with a transition.

## Material and references

- `creator-image-v1/product-in-hand`: establish a creator and product state; derive variations from that actual scene plus product reference.
- `ugc-demo-v1/hands-on`: show contact and one complete operation. `daily-use`: embed that use in an existing daily activity. Both produce silent footage with narration handled separately.
- `talking-head-v1/friendly-explainer`: spoken hook/CTA only with a profile that supports synchronized native speech.
- `product-broll-v1/beauty-hold`: stable product ending or insert. `single-operation`: only when a real change is needed.

Product photos supply form, materials and marks, not unfinished process states. Scene images supply framing and environment. Carry a real previous endpoint for direct continuation; independent inserts can branch from an established reference. Independently generated endpoints do not guarantee matching equipment or geometry.

## Optional review and authorized recovery

Check identity, product handling, the action supporting the claim, persistent state, a readable payoff and coherent voice/music. Review image anchors before motion. Generate accurate brand/CTA text in composition.

Distinguish hard failures (wrong product, impossible required action, false claim, missing result) from incidental director choices (minor background or styling details). Retain user requirements; consider an honest treatment adjustment for an incidental mismatch.

Repair the smallest unit: an intact action excerpt, scene-image edit, failed shot, or timing/layout change. Preserve accepted media and record useful and failed attempts through director cases (`rikoko_guide {topic:"director-kits"}`). This is a candidate playbook, not measured proof of model success.
