# Product advertisements

Use when the product and its visible benefit carry the work, with or without a presenter. Decide whether the audience needs to understand an operation, desire a material/finish, or recognize a use situation. A beautiful pack shot can establish appearance; it cannot establish a performance claim.

## Choose the argument before the shots

Google's action-ad guidance favors a concrete use case, visible product and a relevant ask after context. Its ABCD examples connect the same message across picture, voice and branding. Adapt that principle to the brief; it does not prescribe a universal duration or guarantee conversion. [Google action guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/action_abcds_summary.pdf), [ABCD examples](https://www.youtube.com/intl/en_us/ads/abcds-of-effective-video-ads/).

| What the viewer needs | Useful structure | Evidence the edit must retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand a mechanism | Need → operation → visible changed state → product/next step | Contact, operation and result in a readable relationship |
| Appreciate design/material | Recognizable silhouette → selected detail → contextual scale → clean ending | Stable geometry, surface and recognizable product |
| See everyday usefulness | Specific situation → use → return to the activity | Why this use belongs in that situation |
| Compare alternatives | Matched situation → difference → bounded conclusion | Comparable conditions and only supported claims |

Choose one primary benefit. Record the source of each factual claim in the treatment; missing facts remain missing. Fictional demonstration scripts below are writing examples, not customer testimony or measured product results.

## Reference responsibilities

Product images own shape, materials, controls and packaging. A room image owns setting. A reference ad can supply reveal rhythm or camera movement without supplying the old product's physical behavior. Record preserve/change decisions in the target plan. Read `rikoko_guide {topic:"craft/product-staging"}` before borrowing a liquid splash, smoke burst, breakage, or compression for a replacement product.

Use `product-broll-v1/beauty-hold` for a stable display and `single-operation` for an actual change. A generic product kit is not the whole ad. For a person explaining the use, combine this format with `rikoko_guide {topic:"formats/talking-head-ads"}`; for an everyday encounter, use `rikoko_guide {topic:"formats/ugc-ads"}`. Keep exact sales copy and brand graphics in the edit.

## Authored example: a folding desk stand

Assume the supplied product really folds, supports the supplied phone and has no other verified claims. The message is how it opens and supports that phone, not durability or universal compatibility.

1. Show the closed stand beside the phone, with the hinge unobstructed. The opening question concerns desk viewing, not an invented discount.
2. Hold a three-quarter view while a hand lifts the hinged support. Show the hinge reaching its stop before placing the phone. If the operation will not fit, split at the fully opened state and carry that actual state into the next shot.
3. Show the supported phone at a usable angle. Finish on the identifiable stand with room for the approved CTA.

Direction note: “A right hand raises the hinged arm from flat to its mechanical stop. The base stays flat on the desk. After the fingers release it, the arm remains upright.” Put the state in `initialState/endState`, the movement in `action`, the fixed viewing position in `camera`, and the viewer's comprehension check in `acceptanceCriteria`. This is a rikoko-authored example, not a tested model recipe.

## Judge the argument in the finished work

Can the viewer name the product and the intended benefit? Is the supporting action visible long enough to understand? Do the final sound, image and CTA refer to the same offer? Observe contact and mechanical transitions in motion; inspect branding and geometry in frames. A grid cannot establish load-bearing behavior, speech or sound. Record unavailable observations as unknown. Follow the existing delivery/repair policy; a knowledge entry does not authorize regeneration.
